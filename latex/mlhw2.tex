\question Often (but not always) how are precision and recall related?

% solution goes here
\begin{sol}
  Precision is how overall correct you are and recall is how many true positives you catch. If you focus on recall, you catch more true positives but also end up with more false positives which is less precise but may be better depending on what your predicting.
\end{sol}

\question Between precision and recall, which is a better metric for,
\begin{enumerate}
  \item Predicting good restaurants.
  \item Predicting cancer.
\end{enumerate}

% solution goes here
\begin{sol}
  precision is better for rating non important things so that you get accurate info on something like restaurant ratings. For something important like cancer, its much more important to get as many positives as possible even if it means some may be false, its better to be safe than sorry.
\end{sol}

\question Between precision and recall, which metric is used in the Receiver Operator's Curve (ROC). What does the area under this curve tell us about a prospective classifier?
